線

The Line

Every motif has an ancestor. Hokusai died in 1849, Hiroshige in 1858 — their woodblock prints are in the public domain. We cut out the sky, reduce to four colours and let the cloth take the part the paper used to play.

If you want to know where the wave on your back comes from, the credits name the title, the year and the source.
