Drop 01 · Eleven pieces

Two lines. Both real.

No random characters, no invented history. Every motif comes from an object you can look up in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, and every word is a real term from the Japanese martial arts, with its meaning.

Katagami 型紙

Four hoodies · black · €59.90

Katagami are paper stencils that dyers in Edo-period Japan cut by hand to print kimono cloth. Made for fabric, so they sit on fabric: large on the back, one small grey character on the chest.
